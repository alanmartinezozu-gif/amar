# Wyoming public bidding - reference notes

These notes are background researched by the operator. They help you know what to
look for. **The bid document always wins.** If a document says something different,
report what the document says and cite its page or section. If a document is silent,
say "unknown" - do not fill the gap from these notes.

## Resident preference
- W.S. 16-6-102: when bids are advertised, the contract goes to the lowest responsible
  *certified Wyoming resident* bidder if that bid is not more than **5% higher** than the
  lowest responsible nonresident bidder. Residency certificates are issued by the Wyoming
  Department of Workforce Services, Labor Standards. Flag whether the client holds one.
- W.S. 16-6-104 / 16-6-105 / 16-6-107: preference for Wyoming materials. Some bids say
  these preferences apply and some say they do NOT. Extract which.

## Bid security and bonds (varies by owner - always extract from the document)
- City of Rock Springs: typically a certified check, cashier's check or bid bond of **5%**
  of the bid (W.S. 15-1-113 is usually cited).
- Sweetwater County: seen at **5%** and at **10%**, payable to the Sweetwater County Clerk.
- Sweetwater County School District #1: seen at **10%** when the bid is over $7,499.
- Bids usually cannot be withdrawn for **30 days** (sometimes 40) after opening.
- Performance and payment bonds under W.S. 16-6-112 on awarded contracts.

## Prequalification
- WYDOT road and bridge work: the prime must be **prequalified through WYDOT's iCX
  system**; bids from non-prequalified contractors are not read. The prime must
  **self-perform at least 30%** of the work. Prequalification needs reviewed, compiled or
  audited financials; a contractor with negative net worth cannot be prequalified.
- Some **Sweetwater County** and **City of Green River** road projects require the general
  contractor to be on **WYDOT's Prequalified Contractors List** even though WYDOT is not
  the owner. Always check.
- City of Rock Springs sometimes runs a separate **Statement of Prequalification** step
  before it releases bid packages (large facility projects). Report a "prequal deadline"
  separately from the "bid deadline".

## How local bids actually happen
- City of Rock Springs: sealed bids filed with the **City Clerk at City Hall**, opened by
  the **City Council at its 7:00 pm meeting**. The envelope must be marked with the
  project name and the contractor's name and address. Questions go to the ENGINEER;
  questions less than 10 days before opening may not be answered. The city's Open Bids
  page asks bidders for contact details before releasing a bid packet, so it can notify
  them of changes.
- Sweetwater County: sealed bids to the Board of County Commissioners; awarded at a
  regular commission meeting. Public Works lists projects on QuestCDN.
- SCSD#1: sealed bids, often paper only ("electronic proposals will not be accepted");
  bid questions and answers are posted on the district's bids pages.
- WWCC: sealed bids to the Board of Trustees at 2500 College Drive; often a pre-bid or
  bidders conference; questions less than 7 days before opening cannot be answered.
- Addenda on Public Purchase are posted **only on the platform**. Bidders are responsible
  for monitoring; there are no direct notifications.

## Notice rules
- Public works bids are advertised as **legal notices in the local newspaper**, usually
  twice about 7 days apart. In Rock Springs that is the Rock Springs Rocket Miner;
  SCSD#1 also uses the Casper Star-Tribune.
